**Status: DRAFT, NOT SENT.** Nothing in this file has been sent to anyone.

**Purpose:** establish the status of the two unstarted operations of Budget
Participatif project 11141 before deciding whether a new proposal is needed at
all. This is a question, not a proposal. It deliberately asks for nothing.

**Routing:** send through the Mairie du 7e's official contact page, addressed to
the Direction de la Jeunesse et des Sports. Do not address a named élu until the
current holders of the delegations have been checked against mairie07.paris.fr:
the open dataset this section uses is from December 2021 and predates the March
2026 elections.

**Before sending, check:** that operations 7788 and 8023 still show

## English, for checking what the above says

**Subject: Budget Participatif 2023, project 11141, status of operations 7788
and 8023**

Dear Sir or Madam,

As a resident of the 7th arrondissement, I am writing about winning project
no. 11141, "Favoriser la pratique du sport dans le 7e", selected in the 2023
edition of the Budget Participatif for 500,000 euros.

The city's open data shows that this project consists of three operations, only
one of which has progressed:

- Operation 8022, "Gym douce dans les espaces verts": status LIVRAISON, studies
  began January 2024, delivery expected December 2025, financed in régie, led by
  the Direction des Espaces Verts et de l'Environnement.
- Operation 7788, "Rénovation du Parcours sportif 2,3 km", 23 quai Anatole
  France: status "(non démarré)", no stage date recorded, no financing type
  recorded, led by the Direction de la Jeunesse et des Sports.
- Operation 8023, "De nouveaux équipements légers pour faire du sport en plein
  air": status "(non démarré)", no stage date recorded, no financing type
  recorded, led by the Direction de la Jeunesse et des Sports.

The project is also recorded as LIVRAISON at project level, while two of its
three operations carry no start date at all.

I would be grateful for clarification on the following:

1. What is the current status of operations 7788 and 8023?
2. Has a financing route been settled for either?
3. Has a corresponding programme authorisation been opened, of the type
   "BPA, DJS, 7e arrondissement", and what has been committed against it to
   date?
4. Given that the project is recorded as LIVRAISON, do these two operations
   remain programmed, or was the project closed without them?
5. If the sports trail renovation is still planned, what timetable is now
   envisaged?

This information comes from the dataset
"budget-participatif_operations-projets-gagnants-realisations" published on
opendata.paris.fr, consulted 17 August 2026. If any of it is inaccurate or out
of date, I would be grateful if you would let me know.

I use this route regularly and would be glad to contribute in whatever way might
be useful.

Yours faithfully,

Fergus Leen
Resident of the 7th arrondissement

## Notes on the drafting

**It asks for nothing.** No facility, no money, no site. A request for
information is far harder to refuse than a request for a thing, and the answer
determines whether a proposal is needed at all.

**It cites operation numbers and the dataset.** That signals the claims are
checkable and makes the email routable to whoever holds the file, rather than
reading as a complaint.

**It invites correction.** Question 5 and the closing paragraph both give the
recipient an easy, non-defensive way to reply, which raises the chance of any
reply at all.

**It does not mention the running hub.** Raising a proposal here would convert a
cheap question into an expensive one, and would let the recipient answer the
proposal instead of the question.

**Name check:** the signature uses the repository's git author name. Correct it
before sending if that is not how you wish to sign.
